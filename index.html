What is an image file size?

Images are composed of several dots called pixels, and each of them has a color. To store each pixel, bytes are used. Large images with high resolution take millions of bytes, making them slow to share or upload on online portals.

How can I reduce my image's file size?

You can compress the image or change its dimensions (width and height). QuickResizer allows you to reduce image size instantly without losing quality directly inside your browser!
