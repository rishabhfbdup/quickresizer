What is image file size & dimensions?

Images are made of thousands or millions of small dots called pixels. High-resolution photos from modern smartphones or DSLR cameras contain huge pixel dimensions, leading to massive file sizes (5MB - 20MB). QuickResizer helps you scale down these dimensions effortlessly while maintaining crisp visual quality.

100% Secure & Private Processing

Unlike other websites, QuickResizer performs all image calculations directly inside your web browser using modern HTML5 Canvas technology. Your personal photos are never uploaded to any cloud server, ensuring 100% privacy and security.
