Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Free Services

QuickResizer provides free online tools for resizing, cropping, compressing, and converting images. All standard web features on the public platform are accessible completely free of cost without any mandatory subscription fee.

2. Premium Plans & Billing

If you purchase any optional paid plan or subscription tier on QuickResizer, billing occurs on a recurring or one-time basis according to the selected plan details at checkout.

3. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests submitted within 7 days of purchase may be considered if technical failure prevents normal service usage and our technical team is unable to resolve the issue. Refund requests beyond 7 days of payment are generally non-refundable.

4. Support Assistance

For any billing, cancellation, or refund assistance, please write to us at support@kryzex.com with your transaction details.
