Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Client-Side Processing Security

At QuickResizer, your privacy is our highest priority. All image processing operations, including resizing, cropping, compression, and format conversion, take place entirely inside your local browser using modern web client technologies.

2. No Data Collection or Storage

We do not upload, collect, transmit, or store any of your images or personal files on remote servers or cloud databases. Once you process your file and close or refresh your tab, all temporary image data is immediately erased from your device's browser memory.

3. Web Analytics & Cookies

We may use standard, non-personally identifiable browser cookies or analytics tools solely to monitor anonymous site performance, user experience, and overall traffic patterns.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at support@kryzex.com.
